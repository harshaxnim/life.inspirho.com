Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, New York University, NYC

Harsha Reddy is a CS Grad Student at Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, New York University where he has taken courses such as Geometric Modeling, Optimal Motion Control for Robotics and Virtual Reality. He has maintained a cumulative GPA of 3.97. At NYU, he is working in the Geometric Computing Lab with Prof. Panozzo in creating a framework to enable robotic interaction with deformable objects. He has also been interning with Epic Games Inc. since May 2022, working on Unreal Engine’s Chaos physics engine. Previously, he worked as a Software Engineer and a Research Engineer at a robotics firm called CynLr and as a Computer Graphics Developer at Moving Picture Company, where he is credited for movies such as The Lion King.
Harsha’s specialisation and areas of interests are Computer Graphics and Robotics. Here are some of his works.
