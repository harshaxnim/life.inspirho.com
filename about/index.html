I am Harsh, Your Humble Narrator (A Clockwork Orange anyone?!) of this weblog! This site will serve as an archive of all the adventures that I endeavour, the thoughts that cross my mind, the things that I build, the built designs that I find fascinating, reviews of books that I’ve read and movies that I’ve watched and probably a few more thoughts of how humanity could be a lot better! I would call this a miscellaneous weblog because there really is nothing defined in my mind on where I’m going to limit my writing to (a defining characteristic of a wavering mind, eh?).
